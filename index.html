The CSS Box Model

Every element is represented as a rectangular box made of content, padding, border, and margin.

Margin
Border
CONTENT
Text or other elements
Content The text, image, or other information inside an element.
Padding The space between the content and the border.
Border The line surrounding the content and padding.
Margin The outer space that separates this box from other elements.
